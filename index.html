Voice CopyPaste for Windows

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · Applies to Voice CopyPaste 1.0 and later

In short

  • The app works fully offline. It never connects to the internet.
  • Your voice is recognized on your PC and is never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • What you copy stays in your PC's memory and is never saved to disk by the app.
  • The developer receives no data from the app. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

1. Who we are

Voice CopyPaste is developed by Paolo Panicali, publishing on the Microsoft Store as “ppanicali”. For any privacy question, write to the address in section 9.

2. Data the app does not collect

Voice CopyPaste does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no network permission in its Windows package and blocks network access in its own code at startup. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or telemetry components.

3. Microphone

The app uses the microphone to recognize a small set of English voice commands such as “copy”, “paste” and “switch window”. Speech recognition runs entirely on your PC with an offline engine (Vosk). Audio is processed in memory a fraction of a second at a time and then discarded. It is never written to a file and never leaves your computer.

You can turn microphone access off at any time in Windows Settings › Privacy & security › Microphone.

4. Text you copy

The app keeps a short history of the text you copy so you can paste it again by number. This history exists only in memory while the app is running and is cleared when you close it. The app does not save it to disk.

Copying and pasting go through the Windows clipboard. If you have turned on Windows clipboard history or clipboard sync across devices, those are Windows features governed by Microsoft’s settings, not by this app.

5. Files stored on your PC

The app stores two kinds of files in its own data folder on your PC:

  • Settings: window preferences such as theme and history size.
  • Diagnostic log: the time of each recognized command, audio input levels and error messages. The log records only the length of copied text and of window titles, never their content. It is limited to about 4 MB and old entries are deleted automatically.

The folder is %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\ppanicali.VoiceCopyPaste_n3d77pxdbga4e\LocalState. Windows deletes it when you uninstall the app.

6. Sending the log file

The app never sends the log by itself. If you report a problem, you may choose to attach the log file to an email to the developer. It will be used only to fix the problem and deleted afterwards.

7. Microsoft Store

Microsoft may collect data when you download, install or update the app through the Microsoft Store, such as installation and crash information. That data is collected by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The developer may see aggregated, anonymous statistics from Microsoft, such as download counts.

8. Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.
